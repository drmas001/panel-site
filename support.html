Common questions

Does Panel need an internet connection? No. All content is bundled with the app. Only buying or restoring a subscription needs a connection.

Do I need an account? No. Your progress is stored on your device.

Will my progress transfer to a new device? Not by sync: sync between devices is not in 1.0, and it is planned, with iPad, for version 1.1. Setting up a new iPhone from your old iPhone's backup brings your progress with it.

Which specialties does Panel cover? Ten: Cardiology, Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, General Medicine, Haematology & Oncology, Infectious Diseases, Nephrology, Neurology, Respiratory & Critical Care and Rheumatology. Every specialty is on either plan.
