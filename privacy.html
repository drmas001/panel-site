Privacy policy

Panel Board Revision · last updated 3 September 2026

The short version

Panel does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your answers, scores, bookmarks, flashcard reviews, notes and highlights.
  • Your settings, such as appearance and text size.
  • An in-progress practice session so you can continue where you left off.

This data is stored locally in the app's private storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-in and no personal information is requested.
  • No network requests: the app works fully offline and all content is bundled with it.
  • No tracking, analytics, crash-reporting or advertising frameworks.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or location.

Children

Panel is a professional study tool for physicians and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds any feature that handles data differently — for example optional iCloud sync — this page will be updated before that version is released.
