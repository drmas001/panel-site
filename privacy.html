Privacy Policy

Panel Board Revision · Effective: 8 October 2026 · Last updated: 8 October 2026

Panel is a study app for the internal medicine board examination. This policy says what Panel keeps, where it keeps it, and what leaves your device. In short: there is no account, your study stays on your device, and nothing is sent to us.

No account

Panel has no sign-in, no email address and no profile. We never learn your name or any way to contact you. Panel has no server of its own, and it uses no analytics, advertising or tracking service.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in Panel is kept on your iPhone, in Panel's own storage:

  • your answers to questions, your timed sittings and the sitting under way;
  • your cards' review schedule and their review history;
  • your highlights, notes and bookmarks;
  • the rapid-review facts and mnemonics you mark;
  • your settings — the exam date, cards a day, the daily reminder's time, appearance and reading size.

None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. The content you study — lectures, questions, cards and images — comes inside the app and is not personal data.

iCloud

Device backup. If iCloud Backup (or a backup to your computer) is on for your iPhone, Panel's storage is included in that backup, as Apple's backup includes any app's. Apple holds that backup under your Apple Account; we cannot read it.

No sync. Panel does not sync your study between devices. If a later version offers sync through your own iCloud, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

The free trial's dates. So that deleting and reinstalling Panel does not restart the free trial, the day the trial began and the day it ended are kept in your iCloud key-value storage (under your Apple Account, readable only by Panel) and in your iPhone's keychain, together with the last time Panel was opened on this iPhone. These are dates only; they say nothing about what you studied.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily review reminder, Panel asks your permission and then schedules the reminder on your iPhone itself. It is never sent from a server. Panel schedules it only for the days Cards is open on your plan — through the free trial's last day, or to the end of the month your subscription has paid for — and schedules none while Cards is not open. You can turn it off in Panel's Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

Subscriptions and the free trial

Panel opens everything free for 7 days from its first launch. Then two monthly, automatically renewing subscriptions are offered, Pro and Plus; there is no one-time purchase. Purchases are made with Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account's identity. The App Store tells Panel, on your device, which plan is active, so that Panel can open what that plan includes.

What leaves your device

Only these, and only to Apple:

  • the App Store's purchase and subscription information, when Panel checks your plan or you buy, restore or manage one;
  • the free trial's dates in your iCloud key-value storage, as above;
  • your device backup, if you have it on, as above;
  • crash reports and app-usage statistics, only if you chose to share them with app developers in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements). Apple collects these and shows them to us in summary; they do not identify you.

Links in Panel — this policy, Apple's terms, and an image's source or licence — open in Safari, and what Safari sends is governed by Safari's settings, not Panel's.

How long it is kept

Your study stays on your iPhone until you delete Panel. Deleting Panel deletes its storage on the iPhone; copies inside a device backup follow your backup settings. The free trial's dates stay in your iCloud and keychain after Panel is deleted, so that a reinstall finds the trial already started or ended.

Your choices

  • Turn the daily reminder off in Settings.
  • Cancel or change a subscription in your Apple Account's subscriptions (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).
  • Turn off sharing with app developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.
  • Leave Panel out of iCloud Backup in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → iCloud Backup.

Children

Panel is written for doctors preparing for a professional examination. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

A change will come with a version of the app and a new "Last updated" date on this page.
