What’s inside

How it works

Lecture, questions, cards, then back to the topic that needs it.

  1. Read the lecture

    The case first, your answer, then the explanation. Each page carries its tables and figures. Press and hold any passage to highlight it or write a note.

    Pro and Plus

  2. Answer the questions

    Practise by module, with the answer and its explanation straight after each question. Every sitting ends with your score and the questions you missed. Sit a timed exam of up to 40 questions, 90 seconds each.

    Practice on Pro and Plus; the exam simulator on Plus

  3. Review the cards

    Choose how many new cards a day. Set your exam date, and any card that would fall due after the exam is scheduled shortly before it instead.

    Plus

  4. Go back to your weakest topic

    Once you have answered enough questions in a week, Home names the weakest topic and opens its lesson. Weak topics builds a set of questions from where you are weakest.

    Plus

Common questions

Does Panel work offline?

Yes. Everything you study is inside the app, so it works without a connection. You need one only to subscribe or restore a purchase.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-in. Your answers, notes and progress stay on your iPhone. The Privacy Policy says what is kept and what leaves your device.

How does the free trial work?

Everything is open for seven calendar days from the first time you open Panel, counted on your device. It needs no purchase, charges nothing and does not turn into a subscription. If you subscribe during the trial, the plan starts, and is charged, at once, and the trial’s remaining days stay open.

When the trial ends, only Search stays open until you choose a plan, and a result opens only if your plan carries it. The trial is counted once: reinstalling Panel does not start it again.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account’s subscriptions (Settings, your name, Subscriptions), or from Manage subscription in Panel. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the current month ends. The month you have paid for stays open until it ends. Refunds are Apple’s, under Apple’s policies.

Can I move between Pro and Plus?

Yes. Moving from Pro to Plus starts Plus at once; moving from Plus to Pro starts Pro when the Plus month ends.

Which devices does it run on?

Panel is made for iPhone and needs iOS 17 or later; hold it upright. On an iPad it runs as an iPhone app. A version made for iPad, with syncing between devices, is planned for 1.1. To move to a new iPhone, set it up from a backup of the old one and your progress comes with it.

Which specialties does it cover?

Ten: Cardiology, Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, General Medicine, Haematology & Oncology, Infectious Diseases, Nephrology, Neurology, Respiratory & Critical Care and Rheumatology. Every specialty is on either plan.

Is it for patient care?

No. Panel is a revision aid for a professional exam. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for clinical judgement, current guidelines or a drug’s prescribing information.

I think a question or answer is wrong. What do I do?

Tell us on the support page: send the specialty and the first words of the question. A screenshot of the question and its explanation helps.
